# Manufacturer / brand marks

Unmodified transparent PNGs from HVR88's Monochrome Gaming Logos collection:
https://github.com/HVR88/Monochrome-Gaming-Logos

Pinned upstream commit: `7eb7ddb7246fa5c39b71d5136e7a527bac03fb7f`.
Each filename is preserved from upstream's `png/` directory.

The author permits use in frontends/projects with a note and link back. Original
designs and third-party trademarks/copyrights remain with their respective owners.
The collection's usage disclaimer describes identification/fair-use purposes,
no affiliation or endorsement, and possible restrictions on commercial projects;
it is not a blanket trademark licence. See the pinned upstream README:
https://github.com/HVR88/Monochrome-Gaming-Logos/blob/7eb7ddb7246fa5c39b71d5136e7a527bac03fb7f/README.md

Nintendo, Sony, Sega, Atari, Microsoft and the other available corporate marks
use `publisher_*` files. GCE, Tangerine, Nesbox and Lexaloffle groups use the
source's Vectrex, Oric, TIC-80 and PICO-8 platform marks respectively; Various uses
its Arcade category mark. These are not represented as recovered corporate marks.
The All control is a normal application icon, not a fabricated manufacturer logo.
